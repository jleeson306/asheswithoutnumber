Post the cyber rebellion the governments of North America merged together to attempt to hold on to some semblance of power, forming the United Northern Forces or UNF. It is said they tried to begin exerting authority over some of the previously ruled cities but were crushed by the powerful corporations.

In 20XX the UNF had exhausted any and all resources it had, being little more than a name and a few titles. It was decided to dissolve the UNF let the outer lands effectively rule themselves. This was the final straw for many and the banditry began to soar. Groups that were not sufficiently large and well equipped were forced to re-immigrate to the corp controlled cities.
