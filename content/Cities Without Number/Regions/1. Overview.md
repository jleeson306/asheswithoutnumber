South America
  - Mysterious, with climate change an extreme mist fell over nearly all of it. Many rumors of mysterious things happening with. Any contact attempts fail and expeditions never return. Every so often a new operator will claim to be from these mistlands, often with inexplicable abilities. But even they eventually run too far over the edge.
Europe
  - European union held out on the cyber revolution but was gravely hurt. Was forced to basically become one giant mega-corp ruling over Europe. While this has led to a higher standard of life personal freedom is low. Extremely cut throat business is all they now know.
Africa
  - Brutalized by the effective secondary colonization that was the resource wars. They had their own revolution in tandem with the cyber revolution. This led to a liberation of much the land, leading to few corporate strongholds. However since much of the power is held by former revolutionaries, many governments are military based, with isolation and jingoism being the prodominate trends. However cooperation between nations and inbetween poeples is high.

Australia
  - In Australia the cyber revolution was reported to be particularly bloody with no near end in aight. On the last day if the seventh week of bloodshed, a great storm surrounded Australia, cutting it off from the world. All communication was cut, and travel was near impossible. There are many rumors of what remains in the eye if the storm, from absolute paradise to a pit to hell, or worse. It is said some smugglers run the storm with small fast crafts, bringing back strange almost esoteric prizes. This is dismissed by most as a sales pitch, but some operators swear by this tech.
