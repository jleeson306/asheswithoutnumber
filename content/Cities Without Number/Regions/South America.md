As the resource wars and climate change was heating up, many south American nations began to take a more pessimistic outlook often their lands were the theaters for more developed nations to fight and their resources that were exploited. For a time this led to many nations falling and rising, mergers or lands and people, all with the intent to take control of their fate. Then, when the cyber revolution began, something saw it's chance to take charge. It started gradually in Rio, a thick mist begain to blanket the region. Initially it was claimed to be the fault of climate change, but the mist continued to creep along while ever thickening. Soon the entire continent was covered in this mist, leading to a total blackout from those still within. Since this incident many attempts have been made to explore the land or make contact with the people lost, but no one ever returns. There are rumors some mege-crop have found some way in, but for now it remains a mystery.

Every so often an operator claims to come from the mistlands. These strange individuals often claim supernatural abilities, being on par with cyber led individuals through just their willpower. Will however still can't seem to stop a 12-gage leveled at their head.
