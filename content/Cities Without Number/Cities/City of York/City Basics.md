The city of York is a huge coastal city surrounded by a mountain range on two sides. The city has been though much conflict, and was often the target of attacks. The city had been leveled and rebuilt multiple times, using the previous buildings as foundations for new ones. At least you did if you had the money to not re-use the same damaged buildings. Therefore the richer parts of the city are noticeably taller and contain dense, hard to access undergrounds. If you were to look from the side it would appear as a large slope.

The city is divided into two parts by the Yanos river, with the northside being the most developed.
