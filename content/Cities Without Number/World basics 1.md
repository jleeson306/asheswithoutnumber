Date - 20xx
Global Problems
	- Chrome Cultists
	- Urbanization

(this is mostly true across the world but is mostly about americas)

With climate change resources began to intensify global conflicts. Nation to Nation war was bad however so governments began to increasingly utilize proxy wars using shell corporations to distance themselves from the fighting. Soon near all of the worlds major militaries were privatized, and calling them shell companies was harder and harder.

During this time of conflict cyberware was becoming increasingly common, and it too started to be geared for war. Soldiers were pumped full of chrome and drugs to become killing machines, and often discarded once the side effects started appearing.

Around this time many countries began to push for increased urbanization, relying more and more on automation to handle agriculture and resource extraction. Cities grew huge and dominating, with many of those same corporation have major hands and building them up. For those higher in society this was seen as a golden age, for the bottom majority it was a grind with the best hope of escape being conscription.

This culminated in what was called the cyber revolution. In major cities across the world there were attempted rebellions by extremely cybered up individuals. The main force seemed to be former veterans with military grade cyber. It is unknown how this movement formed and how they came to all attack at once. For a bloody weeks city streets were warzones, pushing many out of the cities and into the wilderness, forming communities in the mostly abandoned countryside.

After the dust settled it was the corporations who had stepped in and put a stop to these revolutions. Reclaiming the cities and either killing or driving away the cyber psychos. Shortly after nearly all cities put out announcements that as the government had failed the cities they would instead now be run my a council of whatever major megacorps had interests in the city.

As the worlds governments didn't have the ability to push back against these corporate secessionists it largely ignored the issue, still claiming to some semblance of rule in the countryside. Attempting to rule over the newer tribes of people who fled the cities and the inevitable raiders and bandits that followed.
